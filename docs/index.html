Privacy Policy — CurlCatch

Last updated: 2026-08-06

CurlCatch is a Chrome extension for QA testers and developers. This policy explains what the extension does — and does not — do with your data.

The short version

CurlCatch collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. All processing happens locally in your browser. There is no analytics, no tracking, no external server, and no account.

What the extension accesses

  • Network requests on the active tab, only while you are capturing. When you click Start capturing, CurlCatch attaches Chrome's debugger (Network domain) to the current tab to read request and response data so it can build cURL commands and replay requests. Capture is opt-in and starts detached; Chrome shows its standard "…is being debugged" banner while active. Capturing stops when you click Stop, switch off, or close the popup.
  • Local storage. CurlCatch stores only your UI preferences on your own device using chrome.storage. This never leaves your browser.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not transmit your requests, responses, cookies, headers, or any captured data to us or any third party.
  • We do not collect analytics, telemetry, or personal information.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not sell or share data — because we never collect any.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • debugger — the only Chrome API that exposes request/response bodies, required for capture and cURL export.
  • <all_urls> (host permissions) — so you can capture on whatever site you are testing. No captured data leaves your device.
  • storage — to remember your UI preferences locally.
  • tabs — to attach the debugger to the active tab.
  • clipboardWrite — to copy the generated cURL command to your clipboard.

Open source

CurlCatch is open source. You can inspect exactly what it does at github.com/nihad05/CurlCatch.
